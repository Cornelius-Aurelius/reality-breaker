FIELD MANUAL / HOW TO PLAY

MASTER THE RIFT IN FOUR STEPS

01 / TARGET

Catch the symbols

Tap or click glowing glyphs before their outer rings disappear. Each captured signal gives points.

02 / COMBO

Keep the chain alive

Hit consecutive targets for combo bonuses. Missing a signal costs a life in Arcade and Blitz.

03 / POWER

Control the battle

Charge the meter to activate Time Warp or a protective Shield. Golden targets recharge you faster.

04 / BOSS

Break the boss rift

Every fourth wave summons a giant rift. Tap it repeatedly, or press Space, before its timer ends.

Desktop: mouse, keys 1–5 for visible targets, Space to strike bosses, P/Escape to pause, T for Time Warp and H for Shield. Mobile: tap directly on the glowing symbols. Audio starts muted. Training mode has no lives to lose.
